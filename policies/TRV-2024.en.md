# Travel and Expense Policy

ID: TRV-2024
Language: en
Effective: 2024-01-01
Status: Superseded
Superseded by: TRV-2026
Owner: Finance

## Booking travel

- Flights must be booked in economy class regardless of duration.

## Hotels

- The maximum hotel rate is $180 per night in all cities.

## Meals

- The daily meal allowance is $60.00 per travel day.

## Getting around

- The mileage rate for personal vehicle use is $0.61 per kilometre.

## Claiming expenses

- Submit expense claims within 90 days of the end of the trip.
- Attach receipts for every expense over $50.00.
