# IT Security Policy

ID: IT-SEC-2026
Language: en
Effective: 2026-02-01
Status: Active
Owner: IT Security Office

## Passwords and sign-in

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every company account, including email and the expense system.
- Never share your password, including with the IT Service Desk. IT staff will never ask for it.

## Devices

- Company laptops are replaced every 4 years, or sooner if a repair would cost more than $900.
- Report a lost or stolen device (laptop, phone or tablet) to the IT Service Desk within 2 hours by calling extension 4357.
- Personal USB storage drives must not be connected to company devices.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Networks and remote work

- Use the company VPN whenever you work on public Wi-Fi, such as in airports, hotels or cafés.
- Home Wi-Fi networks must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption.

## Software

- Only install software from the Northern Lumen Software Centre.
- Only use generative AI tools with company data if they are listed on the approved AI tools page.

## Phishing

- Forward suspicious emails to phishing@northernlumen.example, then delete them.
