# Travel and Expense Policy

ID: TRV-2026
Language: en
Effective: 2026-01-01
Status: Active
Owner: Finance
Replaces: TRV-2024

## Booking travel

- Book all flights, hotels and rental cars through the Northern Lumen travel portal.
- Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class.
- Flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked in premium economy.
- Trips need your manager's approval at least 10 business days before departure.

## Hotels

- The hotel limit is $210 per night, before taxes.
- In Toronto and Vancouver, the limit is $245 per night, before taxes.

## Meals

- Meals are reimbursed up to a daily allowance of $72.50 per full travel day: breakfast $18.00, lunch $22.50 and dinner $32.00.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Getting around

- The mileage rate for personal vehicle use is $0.68 per kilometre.
- Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed when public transit is not practical.

## Claiming expenses

- The deadline for expense claims is 60 days after the end of the trip.
- Attach itemized receipts for every expense over $25.00.
- Claims submitted after 60 days require approval from a Finance director.
