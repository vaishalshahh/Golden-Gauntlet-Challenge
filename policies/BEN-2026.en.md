# Employee Benefits Policy

ID: BEN-2026
Language: en
Effective: 2026-01-01
Status: Active
Owner: People Services

## Vacation

- Employees receive 15 vacation days per year during their first 3 years of service.
- After 3 full years of service, employees receive 20 vacation days per year.
- After 10 full years of service, employees receive 25 vacation days per year.
- Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year.

## Sick leave

- Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year.
- A medical note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Working from home

- Employees approved for hybrid work receive a one-time home office allowance of $437.50.
- Hybrid employees work from the office at least 2 days per week.

## Wellness

- Each employee has a wellness account of $500.00 per calendar year for gym memberships, fitness classes and equipment.
- The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) is free, confidential and available 24/7 at 1-800-555-0199.

## Parental leave

- Northern Lumen tops up parental leave benefits to 85% of salary for up to 17 weeks.
